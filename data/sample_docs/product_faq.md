# Product FAQ — RoboMower X1

## What is the battery life of the RoboMower X1?

The RoboMower X1 runs for approximately 90 minutes on a full charge, covering up to 0.5 acres of lawn on flat terrain. Battery life decreases by roughly 20% on slopes greater than 15 degrees.

## How does the RoboMower X1 navigate?

The X1 uses a combination of GPS-RTK positioning (accurate to within 2cm) and onboard LiDAR obstacle detection. It does not require a perimeter wire, unlike previous Acme Robotics models (X1 is wire-free; the older M-series required a boundary wire).

## What is covered under warranty?

The RoboMower X1 comes with a 2-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects in the motor, battery, and mainboard. Damage from water submersion, unauthorized modification, or use outside the specified operating temperature range (0°C to 40°C) is not covered.

## Can I use the RoboMower X1 in the rain?

The X1 is IP65 rated and can operate in light rain. Acme Robotics recommends against use during heavy rain or thunderstorms, and the mower will automatically return to its charging dock if rainfall sensors detect sustained heavy rain.

## How do I reset the RoboMower X1 to factory settings?

Hold the power button and the "map" button simultaneously for 10 seconds until the LED ring flashes red three times. This erases all saved maps and schedules.

## Does the RoboMower X1 support multiple zones?

Yes. The companion app supports up to 5 distinct mowing zones per device, each with its own schedule. Zone configuration requires firmware version 2.3.0 or later.
