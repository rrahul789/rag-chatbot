# Remote Work Policy — Acme Robotics Inc.

**Effective date:** January 1, 2025
**Applies to:** All full-time employees

## 1. Eligibility

Employees who have completed their 90-day probationary period are eligible to work remotely up to 3 days per week. Employees on the Hardware Test team must be on-site at least 4 days per week due to lab equipment access.

## 2. Core Hours

All employees, remote or on-site, must be reachable on Slack and available for meetings between 11:00 AM and 4:00 PM in their local time zone. This is referred to as "core hours."

## 3. Equipment

Acme Robotics provides a laptop, monitor, and ergonomic chair stipend of $300 for home office setup. Requests for equipment are submitted through the IT Portal and typically fulfilled within 5 business days.

## 4. Expense Reimbursement

Home internet costs are reimbursed up to $50/month. Submit receipts via the Expensify app under the category "Remote Work — Internet."

## 5. International Remote Work

Employees wishing to work remotely from a country other than their country of employment must request approval from HR at least 30 days in advance, due to tax and labor law implications. Approval is not guaranteed and depends on the destination country.

## 6. Notice Period for Resignation

Employees resigning from the company must provide a minimum of 4 weeks' written notice. Employees in engineering leadership roles (Staff+ or manager and above) must provide 6 weeks' notice.
