Acme Robotics Inc. — Employee Handbook (Excerpt)

SECTION 4: PAID TIME OFF

Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid time off (PTO) per year during their first two years of employment, increasing to 20 days after two years of continuous service. PTO accrues monthly and can be carried over up to a maximum of 5 unused days into the following calendar year; any excess is forfeited unless local law requires otherwise.

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year, which do not carry over.

SECTION 5: PARENTAL LEAVE

Acme Robotics offers 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave for the primary caregiver and 8 weeks for the secondary caregiver, applicable to birth, adoption, and foster placement. Leave must be taken within 12 months of the qualifying event and can be split into two blocks of at least 2 weeks each.

SECTION 6: PERFORMANCE REVIEWS

Performance reviews occur twice a year, in April and October. Managers submit written feedback through the internal "Growth" tool at least one week before the scheduled 1:1 review conversation. Compensation adjustments, when applicable, take effect the first pay period following the October review cycle.

SECTION 7: CODE OF CONDUCT

Employees are expected to disclose any outside employment or consulting arrangements that could reasonably be seen as a conflict of interest to their manager and HR Business Partner. Failure to disclose a material conflict of interest is grounds for disciplinary action up to and including termination.

SECTION 8: EQUIPMENT AND DATA SECURITY

All company laptops must have disk encryption and the company-managed endpoint agent installed within 48 hours of being issued. Employees may not store customer data on personal devices or personal cloud storage accounts under any circumstances.
